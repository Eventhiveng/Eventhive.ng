Meet the entire Africa's payments ecosystem under one roof. Policy makers, regulators, banks, merchants, fintechs, card providers, solution providers, acquirers, processors. The list goes on...

As part of Africa Tech Series, Payments Live annually gathers senior payments executives, merchants, banks, policy makers, fintechs and fraud leaders to shape Africa's payments future.

This is an unrivaled opportunity for leaders that oversee payments and fraud strategy, payments schemes, developing payments architecture and infrastructure to explore key trends and opportunities across Africa's payments ecosystem.

Payments Live is your gateway to new opportunities across Africa's payments market, an unmissable platform to learn, network and showcase your payments solutions.

- Expectations

WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT IN 2027

Insights that cut through the noise

Explore multiple expertly curated sessions on the latest fintech, payments and banking trends, with dedicated sessions on instant and cross-border payments, open banking, digital currencies, fraud and FinCrime and much more.

The global ecosystem under one roof

We bring the entire payments ecosystem together under one roof. From traditional banking giants to digital challengers, key regulators and policymakers, to card providers and processors, this is your chance to connect directly with the industry's most influential players.

Brightest minds across our ecosystem

Hear from C-suite and Director level leaders from Mastercard, Nedbank, MTN, Onafriq, J.P. Morgan, Investec, Flutterwave and more. Access insider perspectives on the strategies driving success in Africa's payments market.

Networking redefined, influence amplified

At Payments Live, we fuel the conversations that keep the industry moving. Dive into 1-2-1 connects, collaborative sessions, thematic roundtables and electrifying sunset hour where meaningful connections are forged.
