Drive your business growth at the leading gathering of Nigeria's solar players

Nigeria Solar Forum has become the gathering point for over 1,000 professionals and 300 organizations, blending developers, financiers, policymakers and energy users across the solar value chain.

Reborn from Nigeria Power Outlook, the forum is where Nigeria's clean energy transition takes shape: 30 GW by 2030, half from renewables, and a market built for commercial, industrial, residential and off-grid solar.

- Insights

2027 Key Content Tracks

Commercial & Industrial Solar

This track will explore the booming commercial and industrial solar market, as businesses seek independence from an unreliable grid.

Mini-grids & Off-grid

This track will examine the rapid uptake of mini-grids and off-grid systems in underserved communities.

Energy Finance

This track will unpack the financing models unlocking rural and urban solar adoption, and the investment case for Nigeria's clean energy transition.

Policy & Regulation

This track will focus on the policy reforms, including the Electricity Act 2023, that are reshaping how renewable energy is deployed nationwide.

Energy Storage

This track will highlight storage and digital solutions redefining reliability across Nigeria's solar projects.

Residential Solar

This track will look at how residential solar is powering homes and communities as Nigeria scales its renewable targets.

Energy Efficiency & Management

This track will cover how energy efficiency and management sit alongside solar for homes, industry and the wider power system.
