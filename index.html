- Insights

2027 Key Content Tracks

Payments & Remittances

The evolving landscape of domestic and cross-border payments across East Africa, including real-time transfers, mobile money, diaspora remittances, and the role of fintechs in driving financial inclusion.

Regulation

The regulatory ecosystem shaping East Africa's fintech sector, from policy reforms and sandbox frameworks to the balance between innovation and consumer protection.

Crypto & DeFi

Cryptocurrencies and decentralized finance across East Africa, covering peer-to-peer trading, blockchain adoption, regulatory responses, and the opportunities and risks inside the DeFi ecosystem.

Lending & Credit

Digital lending across the region, including credit scoring, buy-now-pay-later, micro-lending, and the discipline of responsible lending and recovery.

AI, Fraud & Security

How artificial intelligence is strengthening fraud detection, cybersecurity, and risk mitigation, and the ethical questions that come with it.

Embedded Finance

How financial services are being built into non-financial platforms, including APIs, partnerships, and new ways of distributing financial products.

Wealth, Investment & Asset Management

Digital savings, wealth management, and investing, including robo-advice and wider access to financial markets.

Agent Banking

The role of agent networks in taking financial services further, from operating models and technology to the rules that keep those networks working.

Customer Experience

How teams are using design, personalisation, and omnichannel service to improve engagement, retention, and satisfaction.
