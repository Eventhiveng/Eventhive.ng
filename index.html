- The Lagos Transport Fest

Does your work resonate with Nigeria's transport market?

Now in its 8th year, Lagos Transport Fest annually gathers to shape the future of Nigeria's transport market — convening industry players across road mobility, rail, maritime, logistics and supply chain verticals to define the industry's future, address key developments and uncover opportunities.

Combining conferences, exhibitions and networking, the event annually creates a veritable platform that promotes engagement between players, investors and government regulators across the industry.

  • Curated conference tracks across 8 sub-industries
  • Flagship automotive expo at a new venue
  • Direct access to policymakers & C-suite leaders
