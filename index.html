About

Best of Nigeria's tech?
It all starts here

Now in its 7th year, Lagos Tech Fest annually gathers to shape the future of Nigeria's tech ecosystem.

Convening enterprise tech leaders, emerging startups, innovators, investors, tech enthusiasts, global tech giants, new entrants and government representatives, this is where we define our tech ecosystem future, address key developments and uncover countless opportunities within the Nigerian and African tech market.

Combining conferences, roundtables, industry party, pitch live, exhibitions and networking, the event annually creates a veritable platform that seeks to drive investments into the ecosystem.

The future of Africa's tech runs through Lagos — and we converge every year to define it.

Lagos Tech Fest is where historymakers gather to define what's next. The energy is unmatched, the ideas are game-changing, and the opportunities? Limitless.

Whether you're here to unlock exclusive insights, make the deal that changes everything, or raise your profile on the biggest stage across Nigeria's tech, this is where it all happens. No noise. No filler. Just business — done effortlessly.
